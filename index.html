Completed work

In eight days we turned OctaBiz's accounting into a single country-neutral core with a paid accounting module per country, and put that product line on sale.

How it is built

Technical analysis of the accounting module

The accounting module, area by area. Every point below is backed by working code in production.

Country architecture (this update)

  • A country-neutral core. The business's country decides which rules apply; the module unlocks the paid features.
  • One definition per module, checked automatically. One country per business, enforced by the database.
  • Modules included for existing customers stay on. Screens fall back to the country rule if the server cannot answer.
  • A new market is a new module definition and its content, not a new edition of the product.

One ledger for the whole business

  • The till, OctaShop, WooCommerce, Square and Stripe post sales, refunds, tips and payouts into the same books.
  • Point-of-sale sales made offline are queued and posted once when the connection returns, up to 14 days back.
  • The database stops a payment arriving from QuickBooks, Plaid, Square or Stripe from being booked twice.

Controls held by the database

  • Posted bills cannot be edited, and a closed period refuses new entries.
  • Lock dates by area with time-limited exceptions, plus an automatic daily close.
  • An audit log on every table that holds money. Two-step sign-in extends to the books when the business requires it.
  • Multi-step approvals for bills, payments and credit notes, with separation of duties.

Tax

  • Rates, agencies, codes and groups, with rates versioned by date.
  • US: use tax, backup withholding, economic nexus, exemption certificates, sales tax by state, and tax return line mapping for Schedule C, 1065, 1120 and 1120-S. A Stripe Tax adapter can replace the built-in engine.
  • Oman and the Gulf: country chart of accounts, recoverable VAT per bill, bilingual Arabic and English tax invoice.

Inventory and costing

  • Moving weighted average cost; batches and expiry dates with expiry-first picking.
  • Landed cost split between stock on hand and stock sold; lower of cost and net realisable value; IAS 2 price variance split.
  • Bills of materials and work orders at actual cost. Multi-branch and multi-warehouse stock, with branch tags on journal lines and a job that reconciles the ledger to stock.

Receivables and payables

  • Estimates with progress invoicing, recurring invoices with automatic charge, deposits and retainers, payment links, early-payment discounts, customer portal.
  • Requisitions, requests for quote, purchase orders, receiving, vendor returns, EDI orders, bill OCR, vendor portal, scheduled and batch payments, checks, ACH files, 1099 forms and W-9 requests.
  • Goods on approval and consignment-in with owner settlements.

Close, reporting and advanced accounting

  • Close workspace with tasks, blockers and sign-offs, and a fiscal year wizard.
  • Profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, changes in equity, general ledger, aging, and trial balance on accrual or cash basis with comparatives. Budgets by dimension.
  • Intercompany and consolidation, revenue recognition contracts, deferrals, leases, loans, cash forecast, project profitability and work in progress, and fixed assets with book and tax depreciation.

AI inside the books

  • Ask AI works on the screen that is open, for example filling in an invoice.
  • Every AI change is logged and can be undone. Bank matching shows its confidence and its reason.
  • Receipt OCR and an email document inbox, an AI collections agent with reminder calls by voice, nightly anomaly checks, and AI that reads contracts to set up revenue recognition.

Accountants and payroll

  • Accountant role, read-only books viewer, multi-client accountant desk and CPA year-end package.
  • Moving books in from QuickBooks, Xero or Excel.
  • Oman payroll with Social Protection Fund contributions and end-of-service accrual, posted to the ledger.

Competitive advantages

1

The lowest US entry price, and a full suite in Oman

In the US, $19 a month for accounting with the US module comes in under Zoho Books ($20), Xero ($27) and QuickBooks Online ($38), and Core with the module costs $57, a third less than QuickBooks Online Essentials ($85), with point of sale, inventory and CRM included. In Oman it is priced below Wafeq (12 OMR, about $31) and below FawtaraX, which offers e-invoicing alone.

2

Operations and compliance on one ledger

A sale at the till, a stock movement and the tax on it land in the same books the compliance module reports from. QuickBooks and Xero have no native point of sale, Zoho sells point of sale and CRM as separate products, and Wafeq and FawtaraX cover accounting or e-invoicing alone.

3

The enterprise country model at small-business prices

One neutral core with each country as a module is how Odoo, NetSuite and Microsoft Business Central are built. QuickBooks, Xero and Zoho maintain a separate edition per country. For OctaBiz each new market is a new module on the same product.

4

Customer-first terms written into the product

Payment never changes how a number is calculated, a lapsed module becomes read only and the books stay intact, and customers who were with us before launch keep their module free for life. These terms are enforced by the system itself.

5

US sales tax compliance included

Nexus monitoring, an exemption certificate register and the sales tax report by state come inside the $19 module. QuickBooks and Xero list no nexus tracking, and Zoho needs a separate Avalara integration for it. 1099 forms are filed at $2 per form.

6

Fawtara built in, ahead of the 2027 deadlines

E-invoicing becomes mandatory in Oman on 1 April 2027 for large businesses and on 1 October 2027 for everyone else. Zoho Books, Odoo and Wafeq have no Fawtara support of their own and rely on third-party connectors or say nothing about it. In OctaBiz the VAT return, the bilingual tax invoice, the WPS salary file, the Social Protection Fund report and Fawtara e-invoicing all sit inside the system that runs the shop.

  • یک تعریف برای هر ماژول، با بررسی خودکار. هر کسب‌وکار یک کشور، با اعمال در خود پایگاه داده.
  • ماژول‌هایی که برای مشتریان فعلی هدیه شده‌اند روشن می‌مانند. اگر سرور پاسخ ندهد، صفحه‌ها به قاعده‌ی کشور برمی‌گردند.
  • بازار تازه یعنی یک تعریف ماژول تازه و محتوای آن، نه نسخه‌ی تازه‌ای از محصول.

یک دفتر کل برای کل کسب‌وکار

  • صندوق فروش، OctaShop، WooCommerce، Square و Stripe فروش، مرجوعی، انعام و تسویه‌ها را در همان دفاتر ثبت می‌کنند.
  • فروش آفلاین POS در صف می‌ماند و با برگشتن اتصال فقط یک بار ثبت می‌شود، تا ۱۴ روز عقب.
  • پایگاه داده جلوی دوبار ثبت شدن پرداختی را که از QuickBooks، Plaid، Square یا Stripe می‌آید می‌گیرد.

کنترل‌هایی که پایگاه داده نگه می‌دارد

  • قبض ثبت‌شده قابل ویرایش نیست و دوره‌ی بسته سند تازه نمی‌پذیرد.
  • تاریخ قفل برای هر بخش با استثنای زمان‌دار، به‌اضافه‌ی بستن خودکار روزانه.
  • ردپای حسابرسی روی همه‌ی جدول‌هایی که پول در آن‌هاست. ورود دومرحله‌ای، اگر کسب‌وکار بخواهد، به دفاتر هم می‌رسد.
  • تأیید چندمرحله‌ای برای قبض، پرداخت و اعلامیه‌ی بستانکار، با تفکیک وظایف.

مالیات

  • نرخ، نهاد، کد و گروه مالیاتی، با نرخ‌های تاریخ‌دار.
  • آمریکا: مالیات مصرف، کسر مالیات پشتیبان، nexus اقتصادی، گواهی معافیت، مالیات فروش به تفکیک ایالت، و نگاشت سطرهای اظهارنامه برای Schedule C، 1065، 1120 و 1120-S. یک رابط Stripe Tax می‌تواند جای موتور داخلی را بگیرد.
  • عمان و خلیج فارس: سرفصل حساب‌های کشور، درصد مالیات قابل استرداد برای هر قبض، صورت‌حساب مالیاتی دوزبانه.

موجودی و بهای تمام‌شده

  • میانگین موزون متحرک؛ بچ و تاریخ انقضا با برداشت از نزدیک‌ترین انقضا.
  • تقسیم بهای واردات بین موجودی و کالای فروخته‌شده؛ اقل بهای تمام‌شده و خالص ارزش فروش؛ تفکیک انحراف قیمت طبق IAS 2.
  • فهرست مواد و دستور کار به بهای واقعی. موجودی چندشعبه و چندانبار با برچسب شعبه روی سطرهای سند، و فرایندی که دفتر کل را با انبار تطبیق می‌دهد.

دریافتنی‌ها و پرداختنی‌ها
